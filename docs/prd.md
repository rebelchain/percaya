# Product Requirements Document (PRD): `percaya`

> One buy. One sale. The same price for everyone.

`percaya` runs pooled crypto calls on Solana and keeps every caller's permanent, hardware-anchored record. Built mobile-first for **Solana Mobile Seeker** during the **Dev3pack Global Hackathon (27–29 November 2026)**.

---

## 1. Executive summary

`percaya` is a **Skin-in-the-Game Threads** mobile dApp built natively for **Solana + Solana Mobile Seeker**. It solves three structural problems in mobile crypto call groups:

| Structural problem in crypto call groups | How `percaya` on Solana + Seeker resolves it |
|---|---|
| **Burner-wallet resets:** A slashed or negative-return caller can abandon their wallet and start over for free | **Hardware-bound reputation (`Seeker Genesis Token`):** Tiers 1–3 require binding a 1-per-device Token-2022 `NonTransferable` Seeker Genesis NFT into an on-chain `GenesisRegistry` PDA. One physical phone = one permanent track record |
| **Desktop friction vs mobile chat reality:** Calls happen in mobile Telegram and X groups with 2–15 minute windows; switching to a desktop browser misses the window | **Native Android APK + Camera QR + Solana Blinks:** Followers scan a Call QR in person or tap a Blink in chat to inspect locked rules, sign via MWA 2.0 / Seed Vault, and receive native Android push notifications when the pool buys or settles |
| **Zero skin-in-the-game & selective memory:** Callers shill without risking capital and delete losing posts | **Call-as-a-Thread + on-chain record:** You cannot post a root thread without locking SOL first (`>= 5%` of pool). Replies auto-tag `In Pool · X SOL` vs `Spectator`, and every settled outcome—losses included—stays permanently on chain |

## 3. The problem

Call groups on Telegram, Discord and X distribute trading signals rapidly and carry zero financial symmetry:
- **No skin in the game:** A caller can post a ticker without risking capital—or after buying quietly minutes earlier.
- **Asymmetric exit:** A caller can sell into the buy pressure generated by their own followers.
- **Selective memory:** Losing calls are deleted from chat; screenshots highlight peak unrealized multiples rather than realized net exits.
- **Disposable identities:** Even if an address is exposed, generating a new wallet is free.

Documented market examples:
- **MELANIA (2025):** Insiders bought heavily minutes ahead of public social posts for ~$100M in windfall gains.
- **ZREAL (Jan 2026):** Single-wallet sniper entries turning $285 into $627,000 into retail follow-through.
- **ZachXBT investigations (2024–2026):** Repeated documentation of KOL call-and-dump spreadsheets across Solana memecoins.

`percaya` removes the head start mechanically: one entry transaction for the entire pool, one rule-driven exit transaction for the entire pool, the caller locked inside on identical pro-rata terms, and a hardware-linked record that records net participant outcomes—losses included.

## 4. Actors & permissions

| Actor | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|
| **Caller** | Post a SOL bond, link a Seeker Genesis Token + `.skr` ID, open a call with fixed rules and initial SOL stake (`>= 5%`), execute `buy`, claim pro-rata payout + profit-only fee, withdraw bond after cooldown when `active == 0` | Change any rule after `open_call`, withdraw their stake during the join window, exit before followers, raise above their tier/impact cap, set a profit share above their tier, reset a negative/slashed Genesis record by switching wallets |
| **Participant** | Deposit SOL and withdraw `1:1` during the join window (via Android app or Blink), claim pro-rata SOL/tokens after settlement, act as keeper | Exit individually during `Holding`, receive a different entry or exit price than the caller |
| **Keeper** *(permissionless: bot or any user)* | Submit `checkpoint` once per gap, execute `sell` when two checkpoints satisfy TP/SL/max-hold (earning `0.1%` gross), trigger `refund` or `split` past deadlines | Override the contract-computed `min_out`, sell before a rule is met, alter pool rules |
| **Admin** | Manage allowed token/pool registry, slash caller bonds with a public on-chain reason, update `GlobalConfig` defaults for *future* calls, pause new calls, two-step admin transfer | Touch SOL or SPL tokens inside any `CallPool`, alter rules of an existing call, un-slash a `GenesisRegistry` |

The **caller bond** (held across calls in `CallerRecord`) and the **caller stake** (deposited per call into `SolVault` alongside participants) are strictly separated in the Anchor program, the API, and the mobile UI.

## 5. End-to-end call lifecycle

```text
Open ──deposit / withdraw──▶ (window ends) ──caller buy──▶ Holding ──keeper sell──▶ Settled ──claims
  │                                       │                    │
  │                                       └─past buy_deadline──▶ Refunded (caller negligent)
  └─window ends < min_pool──▶ Refunded (no fault)              └─past hold_until + split_grace──▶ Split
```

1. **Bond & Seeker Verification (`post_bond`, `verify_seeker_genesis`).**
   - Caller deposits `>= min_bond` lamports. Withdrawal requires `active == 0` and waiting `bond_cooldown`; opening a new call cancels any pending withdrawal request.
   - To unlock Tiers 1–3, the caller invokes `verify_seeker_genesis`, proving ownership of a valid Token-2022 Seeker Genesis NFT (`amount == 1`) and initializing `GenesisRegistry` PDA (`[b"genesis", genesis_mint]`).
2. **Open (`open_call`).**
   - Caller passes `call_id`, `window_duration`, `take_profit_bps`, `stop_loss_bps`, `max_hold_duration`, `share_bps`, `thesis` (`<= 280` UTF-8 bytes), and `stake_lamports`.
   - Program snapshots every parameter, the AMM pool pubkey, and `bond_cap = bond * tier_multiple` into `CallPool`. Nothing inside `CallPool.rules` is mutable afterward.
3. **Join Window (`deposit`, `withdraw`).**
   - Open to anyone via native Seeker MWA transaction or Solana Blink until `window_end`.
   - `deposit` reverts if `total_in > rules.cap`, if `impact_bps(pool, total_in) > max_impact_bps` (`200` bps = `2%`), or if `caller_stake * 10_000 < total_in * min_caller_bps` (`500` bps = `5%`).
   - Participants can call `withdraw(amount)` `1:1` until `window_end`. The caller cannot withdraw (`CallerCannotWithdraw`).
4. **Pooled Buy (`buy`).**
   - Callable only by `caller` when `window_end <= now <= buy_deadline`.
   - Verifies token is still allowed, `total_in >= min_pool`, `total_in <= cap`, caller stake `>= 5%`, and live AMM buy impact `<= 2%`.
   - Executes CPI swap of all `total_in` lamports for target SPL tokens into `call_token_vault`, measures exact token balance delta (`tokens_bought >= min_tokens_out`), sets `state = Holding` and `hold_until = now + max_hold`.
5. **Price Checkpoints (`checkpoint`).**
   - Permissionless, rate-limited by `now >= last.at + checkpoint_gap`.
   - Reads the AMM vaults on-chain to compute `quote_sell(tokens_bought)` in lamports and shifts `prev = last; last = Checkpoint { value, at: now }`.
6. **Pooled Sale (`sell`).**
   - Permissionless once `prev.at != 0`, `last.at - prev.at >= checkpoint_gap`, and `now - last.at <= checkpoint_gap`.
   - Valid when:
     - **Take-Profit (`reason = 1`):** Both `prev.value` and `last.value >= total_in * (10_000 + take_profit_bps) / 10_000` (when `take_profit_bps > 0`).
     - **Stop-Loss (`reason = 2`):** Both `prev.value` and `last.value <= total_in * (10_000 - stop_loss_bps) / 10_000`.
     - **Max Hold (`reason = 3`):** `now >= hold_until` and `|last.value - prev.value| <= max(prev.value, last.value) * sell_tolerance_bps / 10_000`.
   - Enforces `min_out = min(prev.value, last.value) * (10_000 - sell_tolerance_bps) / 10_000`.
   - Swaps `tokens_bought` back to lamports, pays `keeper_reward` (`0.1%` gross) to signer, computes profit-only caller `fee`, records `net_return_bps` onto `CallerRecord` (and `GenesisRegistry`), and transitions to `Settled`.
7. **Safety Nets (`refund`, `split`).**
   - `refund`: Callable by anyone if `now >= window_end && total_in < min_pool` (no fault) or `now > buy_deadline` without a buy (increments `negligent` on `CallerRecord` unless token was delisted by admin).
   - `split`: Callable by anyone if `now > hold_until + split_grace` while still `Holding`. Participants claim pro-rata SPL tokens directly with `0` caller fee.
8. **Claims (`claim`).**
   - Each participant claims once via their `Position` PDA (`1:1` SOL on `Refunded`, pro-rata `payout` SOL + caller `fee` on `Settled`, or pro-rata SPL tokens on `Split`). When `claims_left == 1`, the claimant receives `pot - claimed` so zero lamports or tokens remain stranded.

## 6. Settlement & reputation specification

### Settlement formulas

```text
keeper_reward = gross_out * keeper_bps / 10_000
net           = gross_out - keeper_reward
profit        = max(net - total_in, 0)
fee           = profit * share_bps / 10_000
payout        = net - fee
user_claim    = payout * user_deposit / total_in   (+ fee if user == caller; remainder on final claim)
net_return    = (payout - total_in) * 10_000 / total_in   (signed bps recorded on CallerRecord)
```

What goes on the caller's record is always the **net return to participants after keeper reward and caller fee**, never gross pool return.

### Reputation tiers & Seeker hardware gating

| Tier | Settled calls | Avg net return (`sum_net_bps / settled`) | Slashes | Seeker Genesis linked | Max pool cap | Max profit share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0 (Starter / Unverified)** | `< 3` or any below fails | Any | Any (`> 0` locks here) | Not required | `2 × bond` | `500 bps` (`5%`) |
| **1** | `>= 3` | `> 0 bps` | `0` | **Yes (`!slashed`)** | `5 × bond` | `1,000 bps` (`10%`) |
| **2** | `>= 10` | `> 0 bps` | `0` | **Yes (`!slashed`)** | `10 × bond` | `1,500 bps` (`15%`) |
| **3** | `>= 25` | `> 0 bps` | `0` | **Yes (`!slashed`)** | `20 × bond` | `2,000 bps` (`20%`) |

### Protocol parameters (Production vs Devnet Demo)

| Parameter | Production default | Devnet hackathon demo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `min_bond` | `2 SOL` | `0.05 SOL` | Fits quick Devnet testing & 0.08 SOL Judge Faucet |
| `min_pool` | `4 SOL` | `0.01 SOL` | `<= 2 × min_bond` so Tier 0 callers can open immediately |
| `window_duration` | `15 min` – `6 h` | `60 sec` – `10 min` | Short demo windows allow live full-cycle walkthroughs |
| `buy_grace` | `1 h` | `10 min` | `buy_deadline = window_end + buy_grace` |
| `max_hold` | `1 h` – `72 h` | `2 min` – `30 min` | Configurable per call within bounds |
| `checkpoint_gap` | `10 min` | `45 sec` | Fast enough to demonstrate 2 spaced checkpoints live |
| `split_grace` | `6 h` | `10 min` | Fallback window before token split |
| `bond_cooldown` | `7 days` | `5 min` | Unbonding delay when `active == 0` |
| `stop_loss_bps` | `100` – `9,000` (`1%`–`90%`) | `100` – `9,000` | Mandatory on every call |
| `take_profit_bps` | `0` (none) or `100`–`1,000,000` | Same | `0` means hold until SL or `max_hold` |
| `min_caller_bps` | `500` (`5%`) | `500` (`5%`) | Caller always holds at least 5% of pool |
| `max_impact_bps` | `200` (`2%`) | `200` (`2%`) | Enforced on every deposit and on `buy` |
| `sell_tolerance_bps` | `200` (`2%`) | `200` (`2%`) | Slippage & max-hold agreement band |
| `keeper_bps` | `10` (`0.1%`) | `10` (`0.1%`) | Paid from gross SOL proceeds to `sell` signer |

## 7. Feature prioritization (72-hour MoSCoW)

- **`percaya_amm` Constant-Product Program & Seeder:** On-chain `SOL <-> SPL` pools (`tBONK`, `tWIF`, `tSKR`), Token-2022 Devnet Genesis NFT minter, and `move-price.ts` CLI for live TP/SL demo triggers.
- **Expo React Native Android App (`apps/mobile`):** Calm light-theme 5-tab Threads UI (`Home`, `Callers`, `+ Post`, `Activity`, `Profile`), direct on-device Devnet RPC reader & tx builder (`chain-direct.ts`), idiomatic `useMobileWallet` hook (MWA 2.0 + Fake Wallet fallback + 1-tap Judge Devnet Faucet), native Camera QR Call Scanner (`expo-camera`) + Call QR Sheet, and Android local push notifications (`expo-notifications`).
- **Node 24 + Hono API (`apps/api`):** Dual-mode (`DATA=demo` & `DATA=chain`), Anchor PDA indexer, simulating Auto-Keeper (`checkpoint`/`sell`/`refund`/`split`), Ed25519 SIWS auth, rate-limited `/api/faucet`, and SSE live stream.
- **Lightweight Web Preview & Solana Actions (`apps/web` + `/api/actions/call/:id`):** Shareable pool preview URL + valid `ACTIONS.json` Blink returning a ready-to-sign `deposit` transaction + Android App Link intent into the APK.

### Should-Have (P1 — High differentiation, low effort)
- **Telegram Channel Bot:** Auto-posts newly opened calls (with Blink/App Link) and settled outcomes (signed net return) to a live demo Telegram channel.
- **Early-Buyers Scanner:** Lists wallets that swapped into the target SPL pool shortly before `buy`, flagging any wallet directly funded by the caller.

### Won't-Have (Out of scope — Non-goals)
- Leverage, perpetuals, margin, or shorting (spot SOL -> SPL only).
- Leaderboards, win-rate rankings, streaks, points, or platform tokens.
- Claims of "guaranteed safety", "anti-rug protection", or "market-moving power".
- Mainnet deployment with real pooled user funds prior to legal structuring and external audit.

## 8. Honest limits & risk matrix

| Question judges will ask | The honest answer |
|---|---|
| **Can a caller still front-run from an unlinked wallet?** | Yes. Nothing closes exchange-funded burner buys completely, and any protocol claiming otherwise is overselling. `percaya` forces the caller's own stake into the pool's entry price, locks their slashable bond, surfaces pre-buy wallets on every call page, and binds Tiers 1–3 to a physical Seeker Genesis Token. Front-running becomes costly and visible, not impossible. |
| **Can a caller abandon a losing record?** | Not at Tiers 1–3 without acquiring a new physical Seeker device. `GenesisRegistry` binds 1 Seeker Genesis Token to 1 permanent history; moving the token to another Seed Vault sub-account cannot create a second registry. Unverified burner wallets are permanently stuck at Tier 0 (`2×` bond, `5%` max fee) and marked `Unverified wallet`. |
| **Why spaced checkpoints instead of Pyth or TWAP?** | Long-tail call tokens rarely have Pyth feeds, and standard constant-product pools lack manipulation-proof TWAPs. Two on-chain reserve quotes separated by `checkpoint_gap` block single-transaction flash/sandwich triggers while working on any AMM pool. Holding a pool price artificially across an entire gap remains expensive and arbitrageable, and still counts. |
| **Why Devnet AMM pools instead of Jupiter on Devnet?** | Jupiter has no liquidity routing on Solana Devnet, and deploying Anchor programs on Mainnet requires ~3 SOL in refundable rent while preventing deterministic TP/SL demonstrations. Our `percaya_amm` implements standard constant-product vault math on Devnet so every swap, 2% impact check, and keeper auto-sale runs 100% on-chain during live judging. |
| **Is pooling capital with a profit share regulated?** | Likely yes in most jurisdictions (collective investment scheme / fund management rules; OJK supervision in Indonesia as of Jan 2026). `percaya` is an un-audited hackathon engineering prototype on Solana Devnet, never marketed as legally cleared for public capital raising. |
